C — Three-Row with Translation

Top row: English name ↔ Arabic name + info. Second row: transliteration/meaning. Third row: metadata. Most spacious — also adds the English meaning. Slightly taller per item.

Why these layouts fix the crushing

All three move metadata to its own row, so it never competes for horizontal space with the names. The English and Arabic names share the top row with space-between, giving each side room to breathe.
