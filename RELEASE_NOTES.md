# Release Notes

## Since 3.0.25

### Living Sky — prayer times that follow the day
- The Home hero is now a living sky that moves in real time: the sun arcs from dawn to dusk through six prayer phases, the moon shows its true phase at night (Meeus calculation), and soft clouds drift across. The top bar blends seamlessly into the sky.
- A dedicated Prayer Times screen: page day-by-day, jump around with a month calendar, and track each prayer per day.

### Monthly timetable & PDF export
- Redesigned Monthly view with a pinned header, Gregorian + Hijri dates, Islamic-event tags and expandable rows.
- Export your monthly prayer timetable as a polished, branded PDF — Ramadan-aware and annotated with Islamic events.

### Read the Qur'an your way
- Choose your Arabic Qur'an font — Amiri or Scheherazade New — from Quran Settings, with a live preview. More typefaces can be added easily.

### Other improvements
- Refreshed More menu with new custom icons and a Duas entry, plus fixes and polish.

## Since 3.0.10 (current 3.0.22)

### Qaida Reader — learn to read the Qur'an
- A gentle, child-friendly course that teaches reading the Qur'an letter by letter across 17 lessons laid out on a journey map.
- Tap any tile to hear its sound; "Play line" plays a whole row back-to-back.
- Earn up to 3 stars per lesson; completing a lesson unlocks the next, with a celebratory, fully Canvas-drawn (no emoji) lesson-complete moment.
- Letter explorer with all 29 letters and their joining forms.
- Resume support: sounds you've already heard are marked with a check and a teal outline, so a returning learner sees exactly where they left off. Finished lessons can be reopened any time to practise again.
- Reset journey: clears all stars and progress back to Lesson 1 so another child in the family can start fresh.
- Fully localised (English, German, French, Indonesian, Malay, Turkish).

### Qibla compass
- Redesigned as a true handheld compass: a fixed dial with N/E/S/W and degree ticks, a spinning gold Kaaba needle and a quieter red north needle. Turn until the Kaaba needle reaches the top "AIM" notch to face the Qibla; it turns green when you're aligned.

### Other improvements
- Fasting now has one smart Tracker tab — Ramadan and voluntary fasts together, adapting to the season, with Makeup kept separate.
- Redesigned Names of Allah, Names of the Prophet ﷺ and the Prophets screens, each with its own theme and calligraphy.
- New searchable Help & About with step-by-step guides in your language — now including the Qaida reader.
- A refreshed Home, plus bug fixes and reliability improvements.
