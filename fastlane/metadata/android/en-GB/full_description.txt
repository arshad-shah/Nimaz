Nimaz is an all-in-one Muslim prayer app and Islamic guide — offline first and 100% ad-free. Get accurate prayer times, read and listen to the Holy Quran, search your whole Islamic library as you type, find Qibla direction, count Tasbeeh, explore Hadith and Dua collections, and much more.

Nimaz brings together every essential Islamic tool in one beautiful, privacy-focused app.
